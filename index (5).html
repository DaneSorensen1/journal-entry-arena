Before the workDr Cash, Cr Unearned Revenue. Later, when performed: Dr Unearned Revenue, Cr Service Revenue.Dr Prepaid (asset), Cr Cash. Later, as used: Dr Expense, Cr Prepaid.
At the time of the workDr Cash, Cr Service Revenue.Dr Expense, Cr Cash.
After the workDr Accounts Receivable, Cr Service Revenue. Later: Dr Cash, Cr Accounts Receivable.Dr Expense, Cr Accounts Payable. Later: Dr Accounts Payable, Cr Cash.

Default when nothing is said about payment: assume cash. "On account," "billed," or "will pay later" means a receivable or payable instead.
